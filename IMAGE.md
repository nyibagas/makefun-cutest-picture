entry.png shows a tiny cream kitten sitting in an oversized pastel-blue teacup.
The kitten holds a bright red strawberry in its paws and is taking a bite.
It was generated by an image model, which produced several candidates.
A vision judge compared the candidates pairwise, and this one was kept because it beat the previous bunny picture.
main.py ships it unchanged, copying entry.png byte for byte to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (default /output).
